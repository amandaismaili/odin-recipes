Cowboy Cornbread

-> Home

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups (213g) medium-grind yellow cornmeal
  • 1 cup (120g) all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1 1/4 cups buttermilk
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup thawed frozen or drained canned fire-roasted corn
  • 2 (4-ounce) cans chopped green chiles, 1 drained and 1 undrained

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F. Place a 10-inch cast iron skillet on the center rack.
  2. Make the batter: In a large bowl, whisk together the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, salt, and baking soda. Whisk in the buttermilk and eggs until combined. Stir in the corn, green chiles, and 1 cup of the cheese.
  3. Finish the batter: Carefully remove the hot skillet from the oven and add the butter. Swirl the skillet until the butter is melted and foamy. Pour all but about 1 tablespoon of the butter into the batter; stir until just incorporated. Immediately pour the batter into the hot skillet. Top evenly with the remaining 1/2 cup of cheese.
  4. Bake, cool, and serve: Bake until the top is deep golden brown, the center springs back lightly when pressed, and a wooden pick inserted in the center comes out clean, about 25 minutes. Cool the cornbread for about 10 minutes before slicing and serving with salted butter, if desired. Store the leftovers, tightly wrapped at room temperature, for up to 2 days.
